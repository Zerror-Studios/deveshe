This storefront's part is **FR-17**: checkout without signing in, sign-in with an email code (also the sign-up), and the order status page. Every new flow goes through the kit, so the store stays in step with it.

## UX as built

- **Checkout (`/checkout`, `views/CheckoutView.jsx`; was `/checkout/[id]` until later on 2026-11-12, see the log).** The view picks the path from the kit's session and `useStoreCheckoutOptions()`:
  - **One flow on both paths (owner, later on 2026-11-12, see the log):** "Continue to payment" validates the form, opens the session (`start` signed in, `startGuest` for a guest), waits for RESERVED and shows the server's final total; "Pay now" is enabled only while that session is reserved for exactly the bag, coupon and details on screen (`isReservedFor` in `utils/checkout-kit.util.js`), otherwise disabled with "Continue to payment to see your final total", and calls `pay()`. Any change to the bag, coupon, contact or email, addresses or the news box disables "Pay now" until "Continue to payment" is pressed again. A closed payment window → a toast and "Pay again" (`retryPayment`).
  - **Signed in:** saved addresses and the kept unpaid order on the kit's `useCheckout().start` → `pay` / `retryPayment`: "Continue to payment" calls `start({ cartId, lines, email, shippingAddress, billingAddress })` (`toCustomerCheckoutInput`; a saved address is sent as its values, as before). The kit keeps the unpaid order (`PendingOrderStore`): on a retry (also after a reload) its saved address is preselected, its pricing shown and "Pay now" pays it again; a changed bag or coupon drops it.
  - **Signed out, accounts OPTIONAL:** `components/checkout/GuestCheckout.jsx`, in the store's checkout design:
    - **Contact (email only since later on 2026-11-12, see the log):** the email (required), "Email me with news and offers" (unticked, sent as `contact.acceptsMarketing`), and "Already have an account? **Sign in**" → `/login?next=/checkout` (the code sign-in merges the bag, then the checkout link opens the customer's merged bag).
    - **Delivery:** the existing address form (`Delivery`; a guest has no saved addresses; first name, last name and phone required, the phone a real number for its country and in India a 10-digit mobile) and billing address, mapped to `OrderAddressInput` by `utils/guest-checkout.util.js`. The guest contact sent to `startGuest` is `{ email, phone, firstName, lastName, acceptsMarketing }` with the name and phone of the delivery address (the phone as `+<code><national>`, `toGuestContact(email, address)`).
    - **Payment:** "Continue to payment" calls the kit's `useCheckout().startGuest({ cartId, lines, contact, shippingAddress, billingAddress })` (the guest bag from `getCart(guestId)`, the kit's `guestCartStore` id) and shows the server pricing; "Pay now" calls `pay()` ("Pay again" after a closed window). The session (and its stock hold) is opened only on that click (PRD-014 W0V2-02). Any change to the contact, the addresses or the bag after "Continue" needs "Continue" again; a coupon change also drops a kept unpaid order (the kit's fingerprint has the lines, not the coupon).
    - Refusals show the kit's message (`getErrorMessage` copy through `utils/api-error.util.js` `getKitKeyMessage`): `INVALID_EMAIL` (scroll to Contact), `INVALID_PHONE`, `SHIPPING_UNAVAILABLE`, `ADDRESS_REQUIRED` (scroll to Delivery), `SIGN_IN_REQUIRED`, `INVALID_GUEST_TOKEN` ("Your checkout timed out; your bag is saved", and the checkout starts over), stock and price answers (the bag is re-read).
    - Loading (button spinners, the page loader while verifying or leaving), field errors per field, a message at the button for an invalid form, an empty or unloadable bag.
  - **Signed out, accounts REQUIRED:** "Sign in to check out" with a Sign in button (back to the merged bag).
- **The bag drawer's Checkout** goes straight to `/checkout/<bag id>` for guests unless accounts are REQUIRED (then the sign-in, back to the merged bag). **`/checkout/start`** (checkout links) opens the guest's own bag the same way.
- **After payment.** A paid (or still confirming) guest goes to `orderStatusPath(statusToken)`; with no token, the old thank-you page. A failed payment of a placed guest order goes to `/checkout/payment-failed`, whose "Retry payment" pays the kept order with the kit's `retryPayment` (the guest checkout token is the kit's, this tab only). The payment-return page (`usePaymentReturn`) goes to the status page whenever the kit has a status token (a signed-in shopper's once PAID; a guest's also while confirming), else thank-you as before. A signed-in payment now goes the same way: the status page once PAID when the kit has the order's token, else `/checkout/thank-you?orderId=…`; a failed payment of a placed order goes to `/checkout/payment-failed`, whose "Retry payment" pays the kept order with `retryPayment` (signed in or guest; "Back to checkout" when no order is kept).
- **Sign in (`/login`, `components/login/CodeSignIn.jsx`).** The kit's `useCodeSignIn`: email → Continue → "Enter the code we sent to a•••@gmail.com" (one input, `autocomplete="one-time-code"`, numeric, paste and autofill; six digits submit on their own) → signed in. "Resend code" after the kit's countdown (60 s, or the server's `retryAfterMs`), "Use a different email", the kit's messages for `INVALID_LOGIN_CODE` and `RATE_LIMITED`. "Sign in with password instead" slides to the existing password form (FR-11), which keeps "Forgot password?" and links back to the code. After sign-in `AuthContext.completeCodeSignIn` brings the store's state in step exactly as the password `login` does (logged-in flag, profile, "some items didn't come along" when the bag merge failed), then `getSafeNextPath(next)`.
- **Sign-up (FR-12).** The sign-up form (`components/login/Signup.jsx`) is gone; the code sign-in creates the account. Name and phone are filled on `/profile` when empty (the existing profile form). The mobile menu says "Sign in".
- **Order status page (`/orders/status/[token]`).** A server page on the kit's `getOrderStatus(token)` (never cached, the shopper's IP sent): "Thank you, Priya! Order #DD1042 is confirmed" once paid (else "is waiting for payment" / "was cancelled"), the number and date, the timeline Confirmed → Shipped (courier, AWB, an https tracking link) → Delivered (date), or Confirmed → Cancelled, the items with images, subtotal / discount / shipping / tax / total, the delivery address as the server masks it, the payment status and the store's contact. A shopper who isn't signed in sees "Sign in with your email to see all your orders in one place" (to `/login?next=/profile`); a signed-in one, a link to their orders. An unknown or replaced token is a 404 with "Contact us" (`not-found.jsx`); a rate limit or outage shows an error with the contact, never a 404. `robots: noindex, nofollow`, `referrer: no-referrer` in the metadata (the kit's proxy also sends `X-Robots-Tag`, `no-store` and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`), canonical without the token.
- **Profile.** The order detail (`/profile/orders/[id]`) shows "View order status" (`useMyOrderStatusToken(orderId)` + `orderStatusPath`).
- **Proxy.** `/checkout` left `loginGuard.paths` (only `/profile` is guarded); `/orders/status/` needs no guard.

## Not done / known gaps

- **Closed the same day (kit 1.1.0 fixes found here):**
  - the news box is sent as `contact.acceptsMarketing` (the backend reads only that for the guest's email consent, never the cookie banner's ads consent); the `subscribeNewsletter` stopgap is removed;
  - the kit now sends the browser ads purchase for a paid guest order itself (from the checkout session, in the tab that paid, including after a redirect provider's return);
  - `pay()` right after `startGuest()` works in one handler, and a failed result keeps the placed order's id. The store keeps "Continue" then "Pay now", so the shopper sees the server's total before paying.
- **Browser purchase events on the status page.** The status page itself fires none (it is a public page the emails also open). The tab that paid sends the ads purchase for guests and signed-in shoppers alike (kit, deduplicated per order); the thank-you fallback sends only the ads purchase (deduplicated), never a GA4 `purchase` (server-side, backend PRD-012).
- **Ads `InitiateCheckout` for guests.** Closed later on 2026-11-12 (see the log): both paths send the ads networks' begin checkout once per page load when the checkout opens with items (`hooks/useAdsBeginCheckout.jsx`).
- Not browser-tested; no run against a live gateway, the kit's live check or a payment sandbox.
